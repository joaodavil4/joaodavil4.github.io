Morf is a curious, affectionate blob made of liquid pearl. Poke it, stretch it, toss it, or pull it
apart until it splits in two, then bring everyone back together. It has twenty expressions, a quiet
Dream room for slow breathing, and a pocket of little moments to discover.

No streaks, no hunger meters, no guilt for leaving. Morf is just happy you're here.
