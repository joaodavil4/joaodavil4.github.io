Morf is a little pearl blob for Android and iPhone. Questions, bugs or feedback:
[jaypisupport@gmail.com](mailto:jaypisupport@gmail.com). Please include your phone model and what Morf was doing.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I pulled Morf apart and now there are lots of them.</summary>
<p>That's allowed, up to twelve. Put a finger on each of two Morfs and bring them together to merge them. With a screen reader, the <strong>Bring Morf back together</strong> action merges everyone at once.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Morf makes no sound.</summary>
<p>Sound is off by default. Turn on <strong>Sound</strong> in Settings. On iPhone, Morf also stays quiet when the silent switch is on.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Say hi doesn't hear me.</summary>
<p>Check that Say hi is on in Settings and that Morf is allowed to use the microphone in your phone's settings. Say hi needs a speech recognition service on the phone; some Android phones don't have one.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>The motion is too much for me.</summary>
<p>Turn on <strong>Reduced motion</strong> in Settings. Morf keeps its expressions but stops the drifting, ripples and moving stars. You can also turn off <strong>Tilt and toss</strong>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I reset what Morf has learned?</summary>
<p>Use <strong>Forget my style</strong> in Settings. It clears the colours and play habits Morf picked up. Your discovered little moments stay.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will Morf be sad if I don't open it?</summary>
<p>Never. Time away doesn't count. Morf is just glad when you come back.</p>
</details>
