> Morf does not collect, sell or share personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** Morf has no servers and contains no third-party SDKs. On Android it doesn't even ask for internet access.
- **What stays on your device:** your settings, Morf's colour, the little moments you've discovered and what Morf has learned about how you like to play. They are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. **Forget my style** in Settings clears what Morf has learned; uninstalling the app removes everything.
- **Microphone and "Say hi" (optional, off by default):** Morf asks for the microphone only when you turn on Say hi. While it's on and Morf is open, Morf listens for a hello so it can say hi back, and for sudden loud noises so it can jump. Morf never records, stores or sends what you say.
  - **On Android**, the words are recognised by your phone's speech recognition service, and Morf asks it to work offline. Depending on your phone and language, that service (often Google's) may process the audio under its own privacy policy.
  - **On iPhone**, Apple's speech recognition is used, on the device whenever your iPhone supports it. Otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy policy.
  - Morf's spoken replies use the text-to-speech voice built into your phone.
- **Motion and charging:** with Tilt on, Morf rolls when you tilt your phone, and it notices when the phone is charging. These are read only while Morf is open and never leave your device.
- **Lock screen and Dynamic Island (iPhone):** while Morf is in the background it can show its mood there. This is a Live Activity drawn by your iPhone; nothing is sent to us.
- **Backups:** your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iPhone) may include Morf's settings and discoveries, under your account with Google or Apple. We cannot see them.
- **Children:** Morf collects no data from anyone, including children.
- **Changes:** if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
